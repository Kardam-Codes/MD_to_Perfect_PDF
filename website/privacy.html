Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 8, 2026

🔐 Our Privacy Promise

MD → Perfect PDF is built with privacy as the foundation. We believe you should have control over your data, especially your documents. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

TL;DR: We don't collect, store, or access any of your data. Everything happens locally on your machine.

📊 Information We Collect

Nothing. Zero. Zilch.

MD → Perfect PDF does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information, document content, or usage data. Your Markdown content never leaves your browser.

🖥️ How the Extension Works

  • Local Processing: All Markdown-to-PDF conversion happens in your browser using JavaScript
  • No Servers: We don't send your content to any external servers
  • No Cloud Storage: Documents are not uploaded or stored in the cloud
  • No Analytics: We don't track how you use the extension
  • No Cookies: The extension doesn't use cookies or local storage for tracking

🔒 Data Storage

Browser Extension Data

The only data stored by the extension is:

  • Your user preferences (theme, default export settings)
  • Temporary browser storage for the current document session

This data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

Document Content

Your document content is stored only in browser memory during your session and is cleared when you:

  • Close the extension popup
  • Navigate away from the page
  • Clear your browser data

🌐 Third-Party Services

We don't use any third-party services that could access your data, including:

  • No Analytics: No Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or similar tracking
  • No CDN: All code is bundled with the extension
  • No External Fonts: Uses system fonts to avoid external requests
  • No Ads: No advertising networks or third-party scripts

🔄 Chrome Web Store Permissions

The extension only requests the minimum permissions required to function:

  • Storage: To save your user preferences locally
  • Active Tab: To function on web pages (if applicable)

We do not and will never request access to:

  • Your browsing history
  • Other extensions
  • System-wide permissions

📝 Document Security

Your documents are secure because:

  • End-to-End Local: Content never leaves your browser
  • No Network Requests: PDF generation works completely offline
  • Memory Management: Sensitive data is cleared from memory when not needed
  • No Persistence: Documents are not saved unless you explicitly download them

🔍 Chrome's Privacy Protections

Chrome extensions are sandboxed and have strict security limitations:

  • Extensions cannot access files on your computer
  • Extensions cannot access other extensions' data
  • Extensions cannot access browser passwords
  • All extension code is reviewed by Google Chrome Web Store

👥 Children's Privacy

Our extension does not collect any personal information, so it is compliant with children's privacy regulations including COPPA.

🌍 International Users

Since we don't collect or transfer any data, our extension is compliant with international privacy regulations including GDPR, CCPA, and others.

📞 Contact Us

If you have privacy concerns or questions:

  • Email: privacy@md-to-perfect-pdf.com
  • GitHub: Create an Issue

🔄 Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how we handle privacy, we will:

  • Update this privacy policy with a clear "Last updated" date
  • Announce changes in the extension update notes
  • Never retroactively change our privacy practices

💡 Our Commitment

We are developers ourselves and understand the importance of privacy. MD → Perfect PDF will always remain a privacy-first tool that respects your data and your right to control it.
